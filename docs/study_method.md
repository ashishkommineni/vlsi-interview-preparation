# Study method

## First pass — understand

Read the question without looking at the answer. Write a two-line explanation
and draw the cycle boundary, transaction path, or object ownership on paper.
Then compare your reasoning with the answer.

## Second pass — speak

Say the spoken answer aloud. Do not recite it word for word. Keep the order:
what it is, why it exists, how it works, one example, and one important trap.

## Third pass — prove

For an RTL or protocol question, point to a self-checking test, assertion, or
scoreboard in the related project. For a UVM question, trace who creates the
object, who owns it, who publishes it, and when it can be modified safely.

## Review rule

Mark a question complete only if you can also answer its likely follow-up. If
you miss it, repeat it the next day with a different example instead of merely
rereading the same text.
